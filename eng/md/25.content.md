## Lamentations 1:1 (id: 10025001001)

1How she sits alone, the city that was great with people! She has become like a widow who was great among the nations. The princess among the provinces has become a forced laborer.


## Lamentations 1:2 (id: 10025001002)

4The roads of Zion are mourning because no one is coming to the appointed feast. All her gates are desolate; her priests are groaning. Her young women are grieving, and it is bitter to her.


## Lamentations 1:5 (id: 10025001005)

## Lamentations 1:6 (id: 10025001006)

6And from the daughter of Zion all her majesty has departed. Her princes have become like deer that have found no pasture. And they have walked without strength before the pursuer.
