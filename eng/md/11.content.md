## 1 Kings 22:51 (id: 10011022051)

51Ahaziah, the son of Ahab, reigned over Israel in Samaria in year 17 of Jehoshaphat, the king of Judah. And he reigned over Israel two years.


## 1 Kings 22:52 (id: 10011022052)

52And he did evil in the eyes of Yahweh. And he walked in the way of his father and in the way of his mother and in the way of Jeroboam, the son of Nebat, who caused Israel to sin.


## 1 Kings 22:53 (id: 10011022053)

53And he served Baal, and he bowed down to him. And he provoked Yahweh, the God of Israel, according to all that his father had done.
